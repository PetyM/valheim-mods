# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First public release.
- Results and bookmarks panels next to the inventory, recipe window with Recipes, Sources, Upgrades and Usages tabs.
- Search with `@mod`, `#tag`, `^ingredient`, exclusion and "or", suggestions, category chips.
- R / U / A over any item (inventory, chests, crafting list), clicking a card selects the recipe at the station.
- Biome of every item derived from vegetation, spawns, drops, locations and recipes; progression sorting.
- Panels can be moved, resized and collapsed; layout survives resolution changes.
