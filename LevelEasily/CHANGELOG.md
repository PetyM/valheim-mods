# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- First public release.
- Level area on the hoe: one click levels a grid-snapped rectangle (arrow keys for size, L to lock to a building piece,
  PageUp/PageDown for fine height).
- Costs the same as manual work: hoe swings, pickaxe hits, stone for filling, stamina (30 % discount), the hoe is
  blocked for the work time with vanilla sounds and effects. Dug stone goes to the inventory.
- Respects wards, no-build zones, cultivated and paved ground, no lava in the Ashlands. Works in multiplayer without
  the mod on the server.
